# Gikomba Market Stall Analysis

**Author:** Esther Wambui Kafuko

**Cohort:** PLP Data Analytics Cohort 7

## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and stock information manually. She needs a clear analysis of how much the business is selling, whether sales are growing, which products need restocking, and where to focus efforts to improve performance.

## How to Run

[Open the notebook in Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/EstherWare/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb), restart the kernel, and run all cells.

## Top Findings

1. Total sales reached KES 46,450 across 14 days with a 10.7% week-on-week growth.
2. Weekend sales averaged 82.5% higher than weekdays.
3. Tomatoes and Sukuma are currently at or below reorder levels and must be restocked immediately.
