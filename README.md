### Gikomba Market Stall Analysis
**Author:** Esther Wambui Kafuko · PLP Data Analytics Cohort 7

#### Problem
Wanjiku wants to know how her fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market performed over 14 days, whether it is growing, what she needs to restock, and where she should focus to improve.

#### How to Run
Open `stall_analysis.ipynb` in Google Colab and select Runtime → Restart session and run all.

#### Top Findings
1. Total sales reached KES 46,450 across 14 days with a 10.7% week-on-week growth.
2. Weekend sales averaged 82.5% higher than weekdays.
3. Tomatoes and Sukuma are currently at or below reorder levels and must be restocked immediately.
